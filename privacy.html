Privacy Policy

Data Protection & Voice Chat AI Disclosure · Effective Date: 14 June 2026 · Operator: Hitesh Khangta
Explicit Consent Required for Voice Chat & AI Analysis By joining the Battlepie network and utilizing in-game voice chat or community platforms, you explicitly consent to the collection, server-side recording, and automated AI transcription and profanity analysis of your voice communications for network moderation and safety.

1. Introduction

BattlePie is operated by Hitesh Khangta, located in Himachal Pradesh, India. BattlePie is dedicated to protecting player privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how it is secured, and your rights under applicable regulations.

This Policy applies across all BattlePie services including Minecraft servers at battlepie.net, the Store, websites, Discord community, bots, and voice chat plugins.

Primary data storage is in India. We comply with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDPA) and make best-effort compliance with European GDPR, US COPPA, and regional guidelines.

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Account and Identity Data

Minecraft username and UUID; IP address upon server connection; voluntarily linked platforms (Discord user ID and avatar); and optional email address provided for store receipts.

2.2 Gameplay and Technical Data

Server session timestamps, playtime statistics, game edition/client version; in-game actions, rankings, and public chat logs; transaction records and virtual goods inventory; punishment and mute history.

2.3 Voice Communication Data

Raw audio captured via the Simple Voice Chat plugin; automated AI-generated transcripts; moderation flags and severity scores; session timestamps and participant UUIDs.

2.4 Store and Payment Data

Purchase records (package name, price, order ID, timestamp); billing country for tax estimation. Note: BattlePie never handles or stores credit card numbers, CVVs, or bank details directly; all payments are processed through secure PCI-DSS compliant third-party gateways.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Service Operation: Authenticating Minecraft accounts, delivering store packages, maintaining leaderboards and statistics.
  • Safety & Moderation: Detecting hacked clients, exploits, abuse, harassment, and enforcing network rules.
  • AI Analysis: Automated audio processing to detect toxicity, hate speech, and profanity in real-time.
  • Legal Obligations: Compliance with Indian cybersecurity frameworks, court subpoenas, and fraud prevention.

5. Voice Chat & AI Monitoring — Complete Disclosure

BattlePie incorporates the Simple Voice Chat plugin for dynamic proximity audio. By activating or participating in voice chat, you expressly agree to the following practices:

What Is Captured Audio transmitted while unmuted, tied to your Minecraft UUID, username, server instance, and timestamp. Muted audio is not captured.
AI Transcription & Analysis Audio is processed via speech-to-text models to scan for profanity, slurs, threats, and policy violations. Severity scores are generated automatically.
Storage & Retention Stored securely on servers in India. Raw audio is purged after 7 to 30 days. Transcripts and moderation flags are retained 30 to 365 days for appeals.
Human Review & Confidentiality Only sessions flagged with severe violations or appealed by players are reviewed by authorized moderators bound by strict non-disclosure.
* Commercial Prohibition: Voice data is strictly prohibited from being sold, leased, or utilized for advertising.

6. Data Sharing and Protection

We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We only share operational telemetry with vetted cloud infrastructure providers (hosting, anti-DDoS, payment gateways) bound by robust data protection agreements.

8. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Depending on your jurisdiction (India DPDPA, EU GDPR, UK GDPR), you hold statutory rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: Request a copy of the data associated with your Minecraft UUID.
  • Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Request permanent deletion of non-essential records.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: Stop using voice chat or request account disconnection.

13. BattlePie Discord Bot Integration

When interacting with the official BattlePie Discord bot, we process your Discord ID, username, and commands to sync player ranks, handle verification, and provide ticket assistance. Data is stored solely for operational utility and can be deleted upon request by opening a support ticket in our Discord server.
